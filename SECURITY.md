# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Only the [latest release](https://github.com/Octaviocossy/wake-up-human/releases/latest) receives
fixes.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities privately, never in a public issue: open the repository's
**Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, or go straight to
<https://github.com/Octaviocossy/wake-up-human/security/advisories/new>. Include the version, your
macOS version, and the steps to reproduce.

Wake Up Human makes no network calls and keeps its Alarms and settings on the Mac, in
`UserDefaults`, so the most relevant reports concern the app's sandbox, its entitlements, and the
release artifacts.
